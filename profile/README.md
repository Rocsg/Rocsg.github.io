# Romain Fernandez

**Plant imaging & modelling — from drone imagery to cellular structures.**

I am a researcher at **CIRAD**, in **UMR AGAP Institut**, **PHENOMEN team**.
I develop image analysis and modelling approaches for cultivated plants.

## Selected work

- **[Fijiyama](https://github.com/Rocsg/Fijiyama)** — multimodal 3D image
  registration and time-series alignment. Creator.
- **[PhenoTrack3D](https://github.com/Rocsg/phenotrack3d)** — tracking maize
  organs over time from 3D reconstructions. Co-creator.
- **Grapevine wood imaging** — research on imaging of trunk diseases.
  Co-lead and research supervisor.
- **Drone imaging in agroforestry** — aerial image analysis at field scale.
  PhD supervisor.
- **Arize** — co-lead and designer of visualisation and analysis tools.
